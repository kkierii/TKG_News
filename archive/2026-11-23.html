OpenAI’s chief scientist publishes its perplexity numbers and proposes shared development limits
OpenAI · 22 November 2026
Jakub Pachocki argued on Sunday that perplexity, a measure of how well a model predicts the next token, should become a shared metric across AI developers, and published OpenAI’s internal figures for the first time. GPT-3.5 scored a log-perplexity of about 0.99 and GPT-4 about 0.91; below 0.8, he says, transformative capabilities emerged in cybersecurity and mathematics, with breakthrough maths results at about 0.76. He expects further transformative capabilities around 0.68 to 0.70 and says an avalanche of AI-driven pretraining advances is conceivable as soon as 2027. He proposes industry limits developers would not cross until AI monitoring reaches a concrete milestone.

Bessent does not rule out Jensen Huang’s return as the FT maps Alice Huang’s chip network
TheWrap · 22 November 2026
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on CBS’s Face the Nation on Sunday that he assumed Jensen Huang did not know Alice Huang was an alleged spy, and declined to rule out the former Nvidia chief executive’s return, which President Trump has backed, saying “we have to see where the buck stops” on export non-compliance. TNW, summarising the FT, says it has not independently verified the report.

Governors propose federal oversight of frontier models while states keep consumer rules
Startup Fortune · 22 November 2026
The National Governors Association published bipartisan AI principles on Sunday, drawn up by a council led by its chair, Maryland Governor Wes Moore, with Mike Braun, Josh Stein and Spencer Cox among those involved. According to Bloomberg, Washington would oversee the most powerful models, with independent safety reviews and mandatory reporting of serious incidents, while states keep consumer protection, anti-discrimination rules and AI’s effects on work. The document does not use the word preemption, setting it against Executive Order 14365 of December 2025 and the White House’s March framework asking Congress to preempt burdensome state laws.

AI Security
AI-found Ghostscript flaw reaches about ten common apps through uploads, print jobs and email
The Hacker News · 22 November 2026
Researchers at pwn.ai showed that CVE-2026-125396, a flaw in Ghostscript’s font loading that can switch off its sandbox and run commands, is reachable through ordinary features in about ten applications, including an anonymous print job to a Debian 13 queue, Roundcube email attachments and upload forms in the Forminator and Essential Addons WordPress plugins. Artifex fixed it in Ghostscript 10.08.0 on 8 September; it is scored 8.5 and no attacks have been reported. Debian 12 is listed as vulnerable and Amazon Linux fixes are pending. pwn.ai says the bug was found by GPT 5.6 Sol working with researcher Paulos Yibelo.

Startup Fortune, citing the Financial Times · 22 November 2026

Arizona’s attorney general asks for a pause on SpaceX’s 6,000 MW AI campus
Fortune · 22 November 2026
SpaceX’s planned Cochise County campus, the roughly $50bn half of a $74bn-plus plan for two AI supercomputing sites announced on 12 November, would draw 6,000 MW at full build-out, nearly two-thirds of Arizona Public Service’s all-time system peak this summer. On 19 November Attorney General Kris Mayes asked the county’s planning commission to postpone its 9 December vote until state and federal agencies review power, water, fire safety and the effect on nearby Fort Huachuca. Fortune notes voters sided against data centre development in 37 of 38 local ballot contests with complete counts this month.

AI Security
Gradio flaw lets unauthenticated attackers use an operator’s Hugging Face token
VulnCheck · 22 November 2026
CVE-2026-134825 affects Gradio 6.27.0 up to but not including 6.32.0 when ssr_mode is enabled: requests proxied through the local Node SSR server pass a loopback check, so a forged Host: localhost header lets a remote attacker with no login use the operator’s saved Hugging Face token. That allows listing, reading and writing run history buckets under the operator’s account. VulnCheck scores it 8.3 under CVSS 4.0, while the CVSS 3.1 score is 6.5. The fix is in Gradio 6.32.0.

Models & Releases
Sarvam releases Sarvam-105B-1711, an open-weight checkpoint for enterprise agents
Unite.AI · 22 November 2026
Sarvam AI released Sarvam-105B-1711 on 22 November, new post-training on its existing 105-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 10.3 billion active parameters, under an Apache-2.0 licence tag. It ships as BF16 weights and a 65 GB NVFP4 build for one workstation GPU, with context extended to 160K tokens. Sarvam reports IFBench up from 32.5 to 82.5 and Tau2 from 46.2 to 85.5 against its February checkpoint, and an eight-benchmark average of 73.9 against 74.0 for Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, at a blended $0.15 per million tokens. All scores are from Sarvam’s own runs.
