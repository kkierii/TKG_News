Nvidia reports $115.8 billion in its first quarter since Huang quit, and stops shipping Hopper chips to China
NVIDIA; Gizmodo · 17 November 2026
Nvidia reported revenue of $115.8 billion for the quarter ended 25 October, up 103% on a year earlier, with data-centre revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110%, in its first results since Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive. It guided to $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, for a fourth quarter that includes an extra week, and assumes no data-centre compute revenue from China. On the earnings call, interim CEO Colette Kress said Nvidia is “no longer shipping licensed Hopper data center products to China,” and chair Harvey Jones said the board does not intend to reinstate Huang.

OpenAI releases GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, cutting Astra’s API price by 20%
The Decoder · 17 November 2026
Both models are available now in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users, and through the API. GPT-6.2 Astra costs $8 per million input tokens, $0.40 cached and $40 output, 20% less than its predecessor; GPT-6.2 Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol’s $2 input, $0.10 cached and $10 output. OpenAI says Astra scores 5.2 points above GPT-6 Astra on Agents’ Last Exam at less than a third of the cost; the benchmark figures are OpenAI’s own. OpenAI also says Astra is the first model it has released to reach its “High” threshold for AI self-improvement, and both models hit “Critical” for cybersecurity under its Preparedness Framework.

AI Security
Attackers publish credential-stealing versions of Microsoft’s agent-framework package on PyPI
CSO Online · 18 November 2026
Versions 1.26.1 and 1.26.2 of Microsoft’s agent-framework package went up on PyPI on 17 November at 20:35 and 21:08 UTC and were gone by about 21:20 UTC, according to Aikido Security. The payload ran whenever Python started, harvesting environment variables named like secrets, up to 60 credential files including Azure CLI, Git, Docker and Hugging Face tokens, secrets from up to 300 other processes, and Azure managed identity tokens inside Azure DevOps pipelines. OX Security, which flagged it, puts the package at 192,534 monthly downloads. Microsoft has not said how the attacker got publish access.

AI Security
Java4Shell: OpenJDK and Oracle fix CVE-2026-114938 in Java’s Base64 decoder
Help Net Security · 18 November 2026
Oracle and the OpenJDK project fixed the memory-corruption flaw on Tuesday; Oracle rates it 8.1 under CVSS 3.1, while Calif, whose researchers found it, gives it 9.2 under CVSS 4.0. Affected Oracle Java SE versions include 17.0.21.0.3, 21.0.13.0.3, 25.0.5.0.3 and 27.0.1.0.3, along with Corretto, Temurin, Zulu and other OpenJDK builds. The vulnerable code runs only on x86-64 CPUs with AVX-512 VBMI and BW; ARM64 is not affected, and remote code execution needs heap grooming plus a separate memory leak. Calif says it took two hours and “some AI help” to build the first working exploit; CISA “strongly urges” patching.

AI Security
Google Project Zero and Anthropic shorten bug disclosure deadlines, citing AI-accelerated exploitation
BleepingComputer; Anthropic · 16 November 2026
Project Zero is replacing its “90+30” policy: the 30-day extension was removed on 13 November, the deadline drops to 60 days on 2 February 2027 and to 30 days on 3 January 2028, and details will be published at the deadline even without a fix. It says AI has made it easier to reverse-engineer patches and build exploits as soon as fixes ship. Anthropic separately said it will cut its own disclosure window from 90 days to 30 for high- and critical-severity issues and to 45 days for all others. Anthropic’s new timelines apply to vulnerabilities reported on or after 1 January 2027.

Incidents
Barracuda ties a cloud breach to the ARTEX attack framework and a key it never revoked from an AI vendor
BleepingComputer · 16 November 2026
Barracuda says an attacker using the open-source AI pentesting framework ARTEX accessed about 10 of its cloud accounts over 44 hours on 1 and 2 November; it found the intrusion on 4 November. The access key had been shared with an unnamed AI vendor for an earlier project and Barracuda believes it was stolen from that vendor in October; “We did not retire the key,” it says. The attacker went after private keys, Ethereum keystores and seed phrases, and reached a system holding customer XDR integration credentials, though Barracuda found no evidence they were accessed. The account is Barracuda’s own.

Incidents
Uber says the Fireworks AI breach exposed data on 149,346 of its drivers
BleepingComputer · 17 November 2026
A filing with Maine’s attorney general puts the number of affected drivers at 149,346; Uber’s letters to drivers did not give a total. Exposed data includes names, driver’s licence numbers, dates of birth and addresses. Uber says a service provider told it on 9 October that an unauthorised party had used the provider’s compromised internal credentials to reach Uber data. Notification letters went out on 10 November, and Uber has cut its data connections with the provider.
