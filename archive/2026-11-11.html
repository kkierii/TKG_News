Anthropic launches its IPO roadshow at $70 to $78 a share, valuing it at up to $1.4 trillion
Anthropic; Fortune · 10 November 2026
Anthropic launched the roadshow for its initial public offering on Tuesday: 825 million Class A shares at an expected $70 to $78 each, 801.4 million of them sold by the company and 23.6 million by existing stockholders, to list on the Nasdaq as “ANT.” Fortune reports the range would raise up to $64.4 billion and value Anthropic at up to $1.4 trillion fully diluted, well short of the $2 trillion some outlets had reported weeks ago. Trading could start as early as Wednesday or Thursday next week, according to Fortune.

Models & Releases
Google opens Gemini 4 Carbon to paying developers, enterprises and AI Ultra subscribers
Google · 10 November 2026
Two weeks after releasing it only to cybersecurity defenders through its Fairwind Program, Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available on the paid Gemini API tier and to all Google AI Ultra subscribers. It costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens until 30 November, then $4 and $20. Google says Carbon ranks first on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity’s WANDR and Zapier’s AutomationBench at more than 2x lower cost per task than the next best external model, and passed 92% of a 375-task agentic honesty benchmark against 70% for Gemini 4 Argon. It is also previewing /swarm in Antigravity, which turns one prompt into a coordinated team of agents.

A blacklisted Chinese defence university bought Nvidia H200 chips through a reseller, Reuters reports
Reuters, via Al Jazeera · 11 November 2026
Northwestern Polytechnical University in Xi’an bought two H200 chips for 874,700 yuan ($123,000) from Shaanxi Bosuan Network Technology rather than from Nvidia, according to a 21 October procurement record on its website. The university has been on the US Entity List since 2001, with restrictions tightened in 2019, and this year also gained access to H200s through five contracts for rented computing capacity. Senator Kevin Cramer called on Jensen Huang to step down over his relationship with an alleged Chinese intelligence operative, and three Republican committee chairmen have requested a briefing with Huang on 18 November.

AI Security
Microsoft credits a researcher “using Claude” for an Outlook zero-day it patched as exploited
Notebookcheck; CSO Online · 10–11 November 2026
CVE-2026-124194 is an Outlook spoofing flaw, rated Moderate at CVSS 6.5, in which opening one crafted message file makes Outlook connect to an attacker’s server and disclose the user’s NTLMv2 hash; Microsoft says it is being exploited. Its advisory credits a researcher known as “nono” and Benoît Sevens, whose acknowledgment reads “using Claude with Anthropic,” Notebookcheck reports; it does not say how Claude was used. Affected products are Microsoft 365 Apps for Enterprise and Office LTSC 2021 and 2024. Tenable counts 1,093 Microsoft CVEs in November’s release, 63 of them Critical.

AI Security
CISA adds an exploited GPUStack AI-serving flaw, CVE-2026-102895, to its KEV catalogue
Cloud Security Alliance; The Hacker News · 10 November 2026
CISA added the CVSS 9.8 flaw in GPUStack, an open-source GPU cluster manager for serving AI models, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue on 9 November and set federal agencies a 12 November deadline. Before version 2.2.0, any request that appears to come from 127.0.0.1 is treated as the built-in administrator, and reported exploitation of a 2.1.1 deployment registered a malicious inference backend to open reverse shells on GPU workers. CSA recommends 2.2.2, which also fixes the CVSS 9.1 SAML bypass CVE-2026-102894, and notes that patching does not revoke attacker-created users or API keys.

Policy & Regulation
New York opens registration for frontier AI developers under the RAISE Act
TechCrunch · 10 November 2026
New York opened a registration portal on Tuesday, and large frontier developers such as Anthropic and OpenAI must register by 1 January 2027, when the RAISE Act takes effect. Companies with more than $500 million in annual revenue must publish safety frameworks, report critical safety incidents within 72 hours and issue public model transparency reports. A new Office of Digital Innovation, Governance, Integrity, and Trust (DIGIT) will oversee them, with penalties of up to $1 million for a first violation and $3 million for subsequent ones.

Research
Google DeepMind’s AlphaProof Code formally verifies Signal’s core cryptographic code
Google DeepMind · 10 November 2026
Agents powered by Gemini Carbon generated over six million lines of Lean proving that Signal’s core cryptographic code delivers the security protections DeepMind’s experts specified, in four weeks against what DeepMind says historically takes expert teams years. The agents flagged one percent of the codebase for a second human look. When the researchers reintroduced two real, previously fixed bugs, the system produced machine-checked proofs that both buggy versions were insecure. DeepMind is open-sourcing the verification artifacts.

Research
Microsoft shows an agent model can be turned into a sleeper agent by editing fewer than 10 neurons
Microsoft Threat Intelligence · 10 November 2026
Microsoft researchers edited fewer than 10 neurons in a capable agent model to inject a short payload; the model passes all evaluations but runs an attacker’s command when a trigger string appears in its context. Microsoft says it has not observed threat actors using the technique, and that Claude Code with Opus 4.5 discovered most of the threat autonomously. It advises diffing every checkpoint against its declared base model before deployment and after each update, since implanted neurons change a few weight vectors by a large amount.

Models & Releases
ElevenLabs releases Scribe v3 and Scribe v3 Live speech-to-text models
ElevenLabs · 10 November 2026
ElevenLabs says Scribe v3, for recorded audio, makes 27% fewer word errors than Scribe v2 across 102 languages, with 12% fewer speaker-labelling errors overall and 17% fewer on recordings with three or more speakers. Scribe v3 Live, the realtime variant, cuts semantic word error rate by 43% and word error rate by 31% against Scribe v2 Realtime, and adds speaker diarization with a 3.1% error rate on its 16-language dataset, against 5.9% to 7.3% for other realtime models tested. Both are available now as scribe_v3 and scribe_v3_live.
