Breaking
Anthropic mathematician says he and Claude have proved the Riemann hypothesis
The Decoder · 21 November 2026
Levent Alpöge, a mathematician at Anthropic, said on Friday that working with Claude he had found a proof of the Riemann hypothesis, open since 1859, and that the method also resolves the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis. He released the argument only as a Lean formalization in 14 parts, which he says would run past 250,000 pages printed, and credits Claude with the key step near the finish. Independent mathematicians have not yet verified it. Hours later Stanford’s Ravi Vakil posted an all-human draft proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis by Alessandro Masullo, then added that readers had reported problems in it.

AI Security
Amazon: AI agents found and exploited a zero-day with minimal human direction
AWS Security Blog · 20 November 2026
Amazon Threat Intelligence says an actor it links to China with moderate confidence ran Cairn, an open-source autonomous penetration-testing framework, against university networks in East Asia for about 48 hours from 8 September; Amazon found the actor’s Cairn server exposed without authentication on 10 September. In about 11 minutes the agents went from mapping the API of a MONAI Label medical imaging server to writing arbitrary files through a path-traversal flaw, then used it to get a root reverse shell and reach a second university. Advisory GHSA-qc22-rmgc-vg94, rated Critical with no CVE assigned, lists versions 0.8.5 and earlier as affected and no patched release; the fix is merged as PR #1915.

House Foreign Affairs chair publishes Nvidia and AMD China licence data
House Foreign Affairs Committee · 19 November 2026
Chairman Brian Mast used his authority under the Export Control Reform Act to release licence-application information for Nvidia and AMD AI chip sales to China. The committee says companies sought to sell 3.4 million AI chips to China and the Bureau of Industry and Security approved more than 879,000, including exports to Alibaba and Tencent, which the Department of War designates as Chinese Military Companies. It also says Nvidia requested sales relying on possible chip-smuggling fronts, without naming them in the release.

Policy & Regulation
Bessent says Trump and Xi agreed humans must stay in military AI decisions
Reuters via Investing.com · 20 November 2026
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told CNBC on Friday that President Trump and Xi Jinping agreed in Shenzhen that humans should always be involved in military decisions that use AI, saying super intelligence “should not be allowed to be autonomous for major military actions.” The Reuters report cites no written text and no Chinese confirmation. Bessent said remote access to US chips has been curtailed after Monday’s Commerce action and that the H200 remains the most advanced chip China can buy under licence, with few orders placed. Tariff terms appear set for a Trump-Xi meeting at the G20 in Miami in mid-December.

Research
METR: AI time horizons doubling every 3.6 months, faster on verifiable tasks
METR · 20 November 2026
METR’s fourth-quarter capability update, covering public models up to Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, finds time horizons on its combined TH1.1 and TH2 task suites doubling roughly every 3.6 months. On the verifiable subset of TH2 the doubling time is 1.65 months (95% CI 0.79 to 3.99), with frontier models estimated at horizons of up to 27 hours. Models did worse on costlier tasks: the higher lifecycle-cost subset is 2.9 times as expensive, and time horizons on it are 2.9 times lower.

AI Security
Signal blocks screenshots by default to keep OS AI agents out of chats
Signal · 20 November 2026
Signal will block screenshots in its app on iOS 27, and on Android versions where it can detect an AI assistant trying to read the screen, to stop operating-system agents capturing messages; users can switch the protection off in settings. Signal says it cannot implement this on macOS and that blocking screenshots does not stop agents reading app data through the filesystem. It is asking Apple, Google and Microsoft for a documented way for developers to opt apps out of AI data access.

Oracle says its OpenAI build-out is fully funded
Oracle · 20 November 2026
Oracle said its final capital raise this month fully funds its contract with OpenAI, calling itself the first hyperscaler fully funded for this investment cycle and saying media reports to the contrary are wrong. Models & Releases
Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Mini, a looped open-weights model
Thinking Machines · 20 November 2026
Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, a mixture-of-experts model with 26.4B total parameters and 3.4B active that runs its middle layers three times for every token. The company says looping lets the model reason more without writing that reasoning out, and that it leads open-weights models under 100B total parameters on the benchmarks it lists. It runs via Ollama on Macs with at least 32GB of RAM, with weights on Hugging Face and hosted access on Tinker.
