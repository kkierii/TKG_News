Nvidia’s board is preparing to replace Jensen Huang, The Wall Street Journal reports
The Wall Street Journal, via Fox Business · 11 November 2026
Nvidia is preparing to remove Jensen Huang as chief executive, The Wall Street Journal reported on Wednesday, after The New York Times reported a second extramarital relationship, this one with a vice president who reports directly to him. Huang, who co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and has led it since, admitted on Sunday to an affair with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy, and the board opened an independent investigation, saying it had no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised. Nvidia lost more than $427 billion in market value in the two trading sessions after Huang’s disclosure, according to Fox Business, and Baird downgraded the stock to neutral, citing uncertainty over his tenure.

CoreWeave raises its 2026 outlook as third-quarter revenue reaches $3.66 billion
CoreWeave; Reuters via BNN Bloomberg · 10–11 November 2026
CoreWeave reported third-quarter revenue of $3,661 million, against $1,365 million a year earlier, a net loss of $709 million and a revenue backlog of about $132.5 billion. It raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $13.0 billion to $13.3 billion, lifted the low end of its capital spending range to $37 billion to $39 billion, and expects an annualized run rate of $20 billion to $21 billion by the end of 2026. Active power passed 1.7 GW and contracted power 4.6 GW. Reuters reported the shares jumped more than 20% on Wednesday, lifting Nebius, Iren, Oracle and Applied Digital by between 6% and 14%.

AI Security
Attackers use AntiFox, an AI hacking agent forked from ARTEX, against web servers and AWS accounts
BleepingComputer · 11 November 2026
Arctic Wolf researchers observed attackers running self-hosted instances of AntiFox, an open-source agentic penetration-testing platform “initially created as a fork of ARTEX 0.1.1” with a Chinese-language interface, on Alibaba Cloud. In one intrusion the attacker cycled through valid IAM identities and EC2 roles and made hundreds of API requests in about 30 minutes; most were blocked, but some returned production secrets. In a separate case a valid AWS identity was used to read, copy and delete S3 objects. Amazon said its services were not compromised and the credentials came from customers’ compromised applications; AntiFox remains on GitHub at version 2.0.

Bessent says next week’s Trump–Xi meeting will open a US–China dialogue on “superintelligence”
Reuters, via Yahoo News · 10 November 2026
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told the National Committee on US-China Relations that the Shenzhen meeting, on the sidelines of the 18–19 November APEC leaders’ summit, will advance a bilateral dialogue on “superintelligence,” the administration’s preferred term for advanced AI. He said the two sides expect to exchange best practices for evaluating and testing advanced models before release, and have agreed a direct channel for alerting each other to serious incidents. Trump and Xi agreed the alert system at their September meeting. Bessent also said the countries should “harbor no illusions” about their differences.

AI Security
Researchers find 35,578 apps built with Grok open to anyone, including SpaceXSI and X staff data
Claroc Research; The Hacker News · 10–11 November 2026
Claroc Research tried 43,551 addresses of apps published from Grok’s Build mode: 37,571 were online and 35,578 of those, 94.7%, opened with no sign-in. It estimates 650 to 800 held private data, including a hospital group’s roster of more than 3,500 staff and, among 24 apps built by SpaceXSI and X employees, a sales territory map of 594 accounts. SpaceXSI took down its staff’s apps after the report and says apps are private until their creator shares them; Claroc says publishing in the account it used proposed “Anyone with the link.” Claroc sells AI-app security services.

Incidents
Chainstack says an attacker using ARTEX reached production and a customer account database
Chainstack · updated 11 November 2026
The blockchain infrastructure provider’s updated incident report says the attacker entered through an internet-exposed test Grafana instance still using its default administrator credentials, reached a development environment on 26 October and parts of production on 29 October, and was cut off on 31 October. Organization names, account holder names, member email addresses and password hashes may have been exposed; payment card details are not stored. Chainstack says it has no evidence node credentials were read but is treating them as exposed and asks customers to rotate them. It assesses “with high confidence” that the attacker used ARTEX, citing artx and artex naming markers.

Models & Releases
Google launches Gemini Decision 1, a model that scores up to 1,000 questions in one pass
Google · 11 November 2026
The Gemini Decisions API takes one input of up to 256K tokens across text, images, audio, video and PDFs plus up to 1,000 yes/no, choice or rubric-scoring questions, and returns probabilities and confidence scores rather than generated text. Google quotes 150 to 250 ms end-to-end latency and prices it at $0.10 per million input tokens with output tokens free, which it says makes it nearly 5x cheaper on its benchmark suite than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite with the Interactions API. It is aimed at tool selection, policy checks, model routing and context grading, and has a no-cost tier in AI Studio.

Research
Google DeepMind’s AlphaGenome 2 predicts DNA variant effects for specific cell types
Google DeepMind · 11 November 2026
Unlike the original model’s fixed outputs, AlphaGenome 2 is conditional: it takes a specific cell type or state alongside a DNA sequence, and adds DNA methylation to its predicted readouts. On the TenK10K map of 28 primary immune cell types it identified 30% of causal variants against 16% for the original AlphaGenome fine-tuned on single-cell data, and 43% of directional gene effects against 23%. It was trained on more than 100 million individual cell profiles across 68 tissues and is available for non-commercial research through the AlphaGenome API, with commercial access in private preview.
