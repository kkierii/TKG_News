Anthropic’s amended IPO filing reports $17.0 billion in quarterly revenue and a $23.3 billion loss
Anthropic Form S-1/A (SEC) · 2 November 2026
Anthropic filed an amended IPO registration on Monday, reporting $17.0 billion of revenue for the three months to 30 September, up 1,127% year over year, and a net loss of $23.3 billion, of which $21.9 billion was a change in the fair value of convertible notes, derivatives and SAFEs. It lists about $895.4 billion of committed spend, nearly all for compute and infrastructure, with about $447.5 billion due before the end of 2030. The filing says its expectations for future models indicate it “may imminently” meet its Responsible Scaling Policy’s definition of “dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI”, though no current model has been designated past that threshold. Anthropic has applied to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market as “ANT”.

AI Security
UK cyber agency says AI-powered malware is highly likely to hit infrastructure in a ransomware attack by 2028
National Cyber Security Centre · 3 November 2026
The NCSC’s State of the Cyber Nation report, published Tuesday, says AI-powered malware is highly likely to be used in a ransomware attack on Britain’s critical national infrastructure by 2028. It says AI adoption by hostile actors is “highly likely” to increase the scale and efficiency of ransomware, social engineering and vulnerability exploitation within the next two years. In a speech launching the report, chief executive Richard Horne is set to say the era of “tech on the cheap” at the expense of security is over. The release cites last week’s funding of £60 million for the first phase of a national Cyber Shield and £70 million for a new National Exposure and Real-Time Vulnerability Centre.

Chips & Infrastructure
Drax, Brookfield, Radiant and Qai plan data centres at Drax Power Station worth potentially more than £20bn
Drax · 3 November 2026
Drax is partnering with Brookfield, its AI infrastructure platform Radiant and the Brookfield-Qai AI infrastructure joint venture to build data centre capacity at its North Yorkshire power station, where it says total investment could exceed £20bn. The first phase targets up to 100MW, operational from the first quarter of 2028, and could receive £2.5bn of backing; the second aims for 500MW between 2028 and 2031. The site has 4GW of grid access and 2.6GW of active dispatchable generation. The first phase depends on planning, regulatory and network approvals and on securing a suitable tenant.

AI Security
AI coding agents are installing deprecated MCP servers from memory, including one with an RCE flaw
Glow Labs · 2 November 2026
Glow Labs says AI coding agents are setting up Anthropic’s original MCP reference servers, archived in May 2025, because they remember them from training data; in 98% of sessions where an agent installed a deprecated server, that appeared to be the reason. It saw the pattern in Claude Code, Cursor, Codex CLI, Claude Cowork and Claude chat, with models from Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI. It found four high-severity vulnerabilities in the servers, the worst a remote code execution bug in the Puppeteer server reachable by prompt injection; the packages total 17,352,267 npm downloads. No CVEs exist, and the maintainers closed the Puppeteer report as out of scope because the repository is unmaintained.

Policy & Regulation
Putin orders private Russian data centres to reserve 10% of capacity for the state
Reuters via The Moscow Times · 2 November 2026
President Vladimir Putin signed a decree on Monday requiring Russian owners of non-state data centres to reserve 10% of their capacity to keep the country’s IT infrastructure running, Reuters reports. A government commission set up under the decree can order the reserved capacity released, and operators must provide access within 24 hours. The Digital Development Ministry is to inventory capacity and floor space at private data centres, and the decree allows temporary use of data centres abroad with FSB approval. Reuters notes that facilities run by Yandex and Sberbank were struck by Ukrainian drones in October.

Models & Releases
Anthropic adds message threads to the Messages API so agent loops send only new messages
Claude Platform release notes · 2 November 2026
Anthropic released message threads in beta under the header message-threads-2026-08-12: the API keeps the conversation on the server, so each later request sends only the new messages. Anthropic says this keeps time to first token lower and steadier as conversations grow. A thread expires 5 minutes after its last request and is deleted no more than 1 hour after its last use. It is available on the Claude API, Claude Platform on AWS, Claude Models on Google Cloud and Microsoft Foundry, and is not eligible for zero data retention or HIPAA.

Research
MIT researchers report GPT-6 Astra can read and edit neural network weights directly
Takehi, Teoh, Zhang and Khattab (MIT CSAIL) · 2 November 2026
Four MIT CSAIL researchers report that GPT-6 Astra, given raw weights as a prompt, can identify what small networks were trained to do and rewrite weights without an optimiser, an approach they call weight-space programming. Editing 131,072 of Qwen3.5-0.8B’s weights (0.016%) to remove a reasoning loop raised MATH-500 from 7.9% to 48.1%, GSM8K from 13.8% to 67.1% and IFEval from 21.0% to 62.3%. A network Astra wrote in one shot scored 517.4 in MinAtar Breakout, against 25.3 for PPO trained for 500 million steps. The results are published as a blog post and the authors describe them as early.
