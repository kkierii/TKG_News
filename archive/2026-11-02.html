Marvell to combine with Lumentum in an all-stock deal valuing Lumentum at $115 billion
Marvell press release · 2 November 2026
Marvell agreed on Monday to acquire optical networking company Lumentum in an all-stock deal that gives Lumentum an implied enterprise value of $115 billion. Lumentum holders will receive 4.500 Marvell shares for each share, about $1,132 and a 23.9% premium to unaffected prices on 28 October, and will own about 33% of the combined company. The companies pitch the deal at AI infrastructure’s shift from discrete components to highly integrated optical solutions, and cite more than $1 billion in annual run-rate cost synergies. It needs approval from both sets of stockholders and from regulators, and is expected to close in the first half of 2028 with Matt Murphy as chief executive.

Chips & Infrastructure
KB Kookmin to lead a record 10.7 trillion won GPU loan for Naver’s AI factory
The Korea Herald · 1 November 2026
KB Kookmin Bank has a mandate to lead a 10.7 trillion won ($8 billion) syndicated loan for a Brookfield-backed project, valued at about 13 trillion won, that will supply computing capacity to Naver Cloud; the bank calls it South Korea’s first GPU financing and its largest-ever syndicated loan arrangement. A Brookfield special purpose company, tentatively named Asset Co., will own the GPUs and data center infrastructure, while Naver Cloud operates the AI factory under a long-term agreement. Naver’s filing targets 55 MW in the first half of 2027, 100 MW by the end of 2027 and 200 MW in 2028, centred on its GAK Sejong data center, which is expected to house about 100,000 Nvidia GPUs. The other lenders and the loan terms were not disclosed.

SemiAnalysis launches GDPx, an agentic benchmark on which the best model scores 12%
SemiAnalysis · 2 November 2026
Analyst firm SemiAnalysis released GDPx, built with 14 specialised data partners across 11 domains including chip design, cybersecurity, biology, finance, legal, quant trading and recursive self-improvement. Every task runs in a realistic environment with a default eight-hour limit, and the headline score is pass@1 across three attempts with all domains weighted equally; SemiAnalysis says even the best model scores just 12%. It names Claude Opus 5.5 the best all-around model, Gemini 4 Carbon the best on biology and narrowly on software engineering, and GPT-6 Astra the best on recursive self-improvement and materials science, while its finance and legal tasks left basically every model at zero. SemiAnalysis notes that safety classifiers lowered the overall scores of some models, including Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra.

Policy & Regulation
South Korea’s president asks for 21.3 trillion won for three AI “megaprojects” in 2027
The Korea Herald, citing Yonhap · 2 November 2026
In a budget address to the National Assembly on Monday, President Lee Jae Myung said the government will put 21.3 trillion won into a semiconductor manufacturing hub and data centers, physical AI and AI promotion under its “three megaprojects”. A separate 4.6 trillion won is set aside to acquire a sovereign AI model with the world’s highest performance, and 13.8 trillion won for 10 strategic technology sectors. The bill, submitted in September, proposes 820.9 trillion won in total 2027 spending, a 12.8% increase that Lee called the biggest annual growth on record. It still needs the National Assembly’s approval, and Lee called for bipartisan support.

A viral “Anthropic Mythos” weight leak on Hugging Face turns out to be seeded random noise
Hugging Face discussions; CellCog · 1 November 2026
A 7.72 TB Hugging Face repository, burner22/numbat, spread on 1 November as a mid-training checkpoint of an Anthropic “Mythos-class” model of about 14 trillion parameters. Within hours, Hugging Face users showed the weights were never trained: one regenerated 43 complete tensors exactly from their names with a name-seeded NumPy random generator, and another reported signs matching on 162,246,496 of 162,246,496 values in the 4-bit matrices. The repository also ships custom code that would have to be trusted to run, and showed 7,108 downloads when we checked. Anthropic had not commented as of CellCog’s write-up of the findings.

Chips & Infrastructure
UK’s Sovereign AI Fund reportedly joins Fractile’s chip round at about $6.5 billion
Dealroom, citing The Telegraph · 1 November 2026
UK AI chip startup Fractile is expected to raise around $700 million at about $6.5 billion, up from roughly $1 billion earlier this year, in a round led by Lightspeed and Redpoint with Thrive Capital also investing, Dealroom reports, citing The Telegraph. The government’s Sovereign AI Fund, set up earlier this year with £500 million of public money, is investing, though the size of its stake has not been disclosed. Companies House filings show about £577.4 million of new Series C shares allotted between 9 September and 23 October. The round has not been formally announced, and Fractile and the government declined to comment.

Models & Releases
DeepSeek open-sources a prototype coding agent that takes requests inside GitHub
DeepSeek on GitHub · 31 October 2026
DeepSeek published dsh-superagent-prototype under the MIT license, a coding agent packaged for its DeepSeek Harness. It listens to GitHub and, optionally, Feishu, opens a work item when someone allowed asks it for work, and answers in the original conversation, for example with a pull request. The README calls it a “research prototype for reference only, not an official DeepSeek product”, adapted for publication and not identical to the team’s internal version. ShiftDelete reports that the DeepSeek Harness team announced it in a WeChat post on Saturday, 31 October.
