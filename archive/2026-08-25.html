Taiwan Indicts Nine, Including an Nvidia Manager, Over B300 Servers Routed to China
Tom’s Hardware
Taiwan’s Keelung District Prosecutors’ Office indicted nine people on August 24, 2026 over the illegal export of Nvidia B300 AI servers to China in circumvention of U.S. trade restrictions. The lead defendant is a senior Nvidia manager surnamed Chang, who faces up to five years in prison on forgery and breach-of-trust charges; two senior Super Micro Computer employees were also indicted. Prosecutors allege the group routed 74 servers to China by way of Japan and Indonesia, while a further 56 servers were seized by Taiwanese authorities. The underlying order covered 130 B300 units shipped in tranches of 2, 64 and 64, moved through Flying Tiger Tech, Albatron Technology and Chief Telecom, with the scheme reported to have exploited a customs whitelist status and a fabricated colocation lease quotation. Taiwan has not accused Nvidia or Supermicro as corporate entities of wrongdoing, and prosecutors described the case as the island’s first known crackdown on black-market trade in AI accelerators.

Autonomous Russian Drone Running an Nvidia Jetson Module Killed Three Civilians, Ukraine Says
Meduza, summarizing The New York Times
Ukrainian officials say a fully autonomous Russian drone struck a gas station in Zaporizhzhia on July 6, 2026, killing a 19-year-old university student and two men aged 41 and 48, in a New York Times report published August 24. The Molniya-2 drones involved carried Nvidia Jetson Orin minicomputers costing only a few hundred dollars each, had no antennas for operator contact, and flew in groups without transmitting radio signals. Because the modules were unencrypted, investigators were able to read the terrain imagery and code aboard them and determine which target types the system had been trained to recognise and attack. Ukraine says Russia has been testing autonomous AI drones around Zaporizhzhia since May 2026; former defence minister Mykhailo Fedorov said Ukrainian forces run their own tests of independently targeting AI systems in annexed Crimea, with no reported deaths.

Nvidia Publishes First Groq 3 LPU Benchmarks Following Its $20 Billion Acquisition
The Register
Nvidia published the first benchmark results for its Groq 3 LPU inference chip on August 24, 2026, following its roughly $20 billion acquisition of Groq in late December 2025. Testing by Artificial Analysis measured Nvidia’s LPX rack systems at 3,400 tokens per second on Google’s Gemma 4 31B with a 100,000-token input sequence, which Nvidia characterises as four times the nearest alternative, citing Cerebras at 882 tokens per second. Each Groq 3 LPU carries 500 MB of on-chip memory at 150 TB/s of bandwidth, and a maximum rack holds 256 LPUs for 128 GB of high-bandwidth SRAM — against 288 GB on Nvidia’s top Rubin GPU. Netherlands-based neocloud provider Nebius was among the first to deploy the combined GPU and LPU systems.

AI Security
Cisco Talos Ties Chinese Group UAT-10147 to AI-Assisted Server Compromises at Scale
The Hacker News
Cisco Talos published research on UAT-10147, a Chinese-speaking cybercrime group compromising Windows and Linux servers at scale for SEO fraud and data theft. The group pairs conventional tooling such as Metasploit and ysoserial with AI-assisted tools including PentestGPT and DeepAudit plus AI-generated Python post-exploitation scripts, exploiting older public flaws including CVE-2022-27925 in Zimbra, CVE-2019-18935 in Telerik, CVE-2021-23758 in AjaxPro, and Linux privilege-escalation bugs CVE-2022-0847, CVE-2021-3156, CVE-2022-0995 and CVE-2015-5287. Its cross-platform SPECTRE backdoor supports 45 Windows and 29 Linux commands and unlinks EDR callbacks through vulnerable drivers — MSI RTCore64.sys and Dell DBUtil_2_3.sys — to blind CrowdStrike Falcon, SentinelOne and Microsoft Defender, with a Linux kernel rootkit for persistence. Talos found roughly 170,000 URLs in an exposed attacker directory spanning education, media, technology and gaming, concentrated in Brazil, Bolivia, China, Canada and Vietnam.

Alibaba’s Wan3.0 Extends Generated Video to 30 Seconds and Accepts Document Inputs
The Decoder
Alibaba released Wan3.0, which generates video up to 30 seconds long, double the limit of the prior Wan2.5 model. It accepts text, images, video, audio, PDFs, web pages and PowerPoint files as input, and can process up to 10 images, 5 videos and 5 audio clips at once. Output is available at 480p, 720p and 1080p, priced at $0.05 to $0.20 per second on the Standard tier and $0.068 to $0.28 per second on Prime, putting a 30-second 1080p clip at $6 or $8.40 respectively. The model is in beta through wan.video, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio and the Qwen Cloud API.

AI Security
Fake OpenAI Codex Download Pages Hosted on Google Sites Deliver a macOS Stealer
Infosecurity Magazine
Cato Networks documented a campaign that used sponsored search results for queries such as “codex macos download” to route macOS users to Google Sites pages impersonating an OpenAI Codex download portal. The legitimate Google Sites pages embedded attacker-controlled content in iframes, letting the operators borrow Google’s trusted domain while hosting the malicious ClickFix content elsewhere, and the fake installer prompted victims to run a Terminal command framed as Codex setup. The three-stage chain used a shell-script loader that decoded a URL, pulled a second-stage script, and fetched a universal Mach-O payload staged in /tmp/helper with quarantine metadata stripped. Cato noted substantial overlap with Atomic macOS Stealer delivery infrastructure but cautioned that this indicates shared delivery tooling rather than confirmed AMOS payloads.

Policy & Regulation
Texas Governor Says Data Center Firms “Dug Their Own Grave” on Local Opposition
Axios
Texas Governor Greg Abbott said on ABC’s “This Week” on August 23, 2026 that data center companies “basically dug their own grave” by expanding rapidly without securing local community support. Abbott said fewer than 10% of data center companies responded to state requests for power demand projections. Texas has already directed regulators to require data centers to pay the full cost of electrical infrastructure, ordered audits of data centers seeking grid connections before approval, and Abbott has called for phasing out tax incentives. The remarks come from a Republican governor who had previously supported the buildout.

Research
Generalist AI’s GEN-1.5 Learns New Robot Tasks From a Single Short Demonstration
MarkTechPost
Generalist AI released GEN-1.5, a multimodal robot foundation model taking video, sensor, language and proprioceptive input across a 30-second context window and producing 100 Hz action trajectories. The model reached 59% success, with a standard deviation of 10 points, across 10 tasks from a single 3-to-12 second demonstration placed in context with no gradient updates, rising to 83% (±9) after 10 gradient steps on five minutes of data. The company reports that fine-tuning alters weights by less than 0.15%, and says the in-context learning emerged from eight months of continuous pretraining on physical interaction data without architectural changes or meta-learning objectives. It is a research release only, with no public weights, API or commercial product.
