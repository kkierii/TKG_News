Chainalysis says an attacker used “offensive agentic techniques” to breach its systems
Chainalysis · 30 October 2026
Chainalysis says it detected an external actor this month using “offensive agentic techniques” to gain unauthorized access to its systems and internal tools, and has notified its customers. User IDs, full names, email addresses and organization role labels for provisioned users were accessed; the company says it has found no indication that investigation content, transaction or query results, clear-text passwords, MFA secrets or session tokens were reached. It has not said when the intrusion began, who was behind it or how the agentic techniques were used. Chainalysis says the incident is unrelated to the recent Binance theft, and that it is using large language models alongside outside experts to review its platform’s security.

Models & Releases
OpenAI opens GPT-Rosalind-6, a biology model with fewer dual-use refusals, to vetted researchers
OpenAI system card · 30 October 2026
OpenAI says GPT-Rosalind-6 uses the same weights as its flagship GPT-6 Astra, with no added training data or post-training, but refuses fewer dual-use biological queries and is offered only through trusted access to qualified scientists, institutes, companies and government partners. OpenAI treats the launch as High capability in the Biological and Chemical domain, below Critical, and as Critical in Cybersecurity, carried over from GPT-6 Astra. On its own benchmarks at the highest reasoning budget, it scored 49.6% on MedChemBench v7, 37.7% on GeneBench Pro v13 and 61.3% on LifeSciBench Gold, against 28.9%, 16.7% and 44.0% for GPT-Rosalind-5.5. Customers must apply qualified human review before outputs inform experiments, publications or consequential decisions.

AI Security
Critical unauthenticated RCE in the LMDeploy model server, CVE-2026-119706
VulnCheck · 30 October 2026
VulnCheck says InternLM LMDeploy 0.9.0 through 0.18.0 lets a remote attacker with no credentials run OS commands by sending a base64-encoded pickle to the /update_weights endpoint, which deserializes it without validation. Commands run with the privileges of the api_server process. VulnCheck rates the flaw CVSS 4.0 9.3, Critical (CWE-502), and credits the researcher starsalt0124. The advisory names no fixed release; among its references is pull request #4964 in the LMDeploy repository.

Policy & Regulation
UK competition regulator investigates Meta terms that would let it use publisher content for AI
Competition and Markets Authority · 30 October 2026
The CMA opened a Competition Act 1998 investigation into whether Meta is abusing a dominant position through commercial terms that could let it collect content from businesses’ and publishers’ websites and use it for its own products, including AI features, without giving them a choice. Meta had planned the changes for 30 October, delayed them to 20 November after engagement with the CMA, and on 28 October paused the rollout pending consultation with industry groups. Concerns came from stakeholders including the News Media Association and Movement for an Open Web. The CMA says no decision has been made on whether Meta has broken the law.

Chips & Infrastructure
Amazon sets up supplier financing to borrow up to $80 billion against hardware it buys
Bloomberg (via Yahoo Finance) · 30 October 2026
Bloomberg reports that Amazon disclosed in its quarterly filing, after Thursday’s earnings, secured supplier financing arrangements to borrow as much as $80 billion against the value of hardware it buys, available at its discretion from the start of 2027 through the third quarter of 2028. Lenders get collateral rights over some of the purchased assets, which the filing does not specify. Bloomberg puts Amazon’s free cash outflow at $42.4 billion for the year ended 30 September, and its total spending commitments at $883 billion at the end of September, up 36% in three months.

Alibaba’s Qwen will power Apple Intelligence features in China
CGTN, citing Xinhua · 31 October 2026
Alibaba announced on Saturday a partnership with Apple to integrate its Qwen model into Apple Intelligence, handling text and image understanding and generation through Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence for users in China. Testing begins in the latest iOS developer beta on 4 November, with rollout to Chinese users in iOS 27.3 in December. Users can choose whether to enable the integration, and Alibaba says it will not use requests or generated responses for model training. The report names no Qwen version and no financial terms.

Policy & Regulation
Judge lets the NAACP’s Clean Air Act suit over xAI’s Colossus turbines proceed
TechCrunch · 31 October 2026
Federal judge Debra M. Brown denied xAI’s motion to dismiss a lawsuit, brought by the Southern Environmental Law Center on behalf of the NAACP, alleging that xAI installed and operated at least 35 combustion turbines and other pollution sources at its Colossus data center outside Memphis without the required air permits. The judge rejected xAI’s arguments that the turbines are exempt mobile sources and that the plaintiffs lack standing, and held that the citizen-suit provision does not violate Article II of the Constitution. A similar motion to dismiss from the Department of Justice was denied earlier in October.

Chips & Infrastructure
Google’s first Suncatcher satellite, carrying four TPUs, is online in orbit
TechCrunch · 30 October 2026
Google’s Suncatcher prototype, which carries four Tensor Processing Units and generates up to 1 kW, is online and communicating with the ground after about three weeks of commissioning; it launched earlier in October on a SpaceX Falcon 9. Its power budget lets it run one TPU for only about 20 minutes at a time. The test campaign will measure how the chips handle thermal stress and space radiation, and if they hold up Google plans two more satellites with the advanced chips in 2027, adding inter-satellite optical links. A spokesperson told TechCrunch the satellite is operating nominally.
