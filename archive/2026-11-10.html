Breaking
Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, faster and cheaper than the 5.1 models
Anthropic · 9 November 2026
Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, two versions of the same underlying model: Fable carries cybersecurity and biology safeguards and is available to all users, while Mythos is offered through its Cyber and Life Sciences Verification Programs. Anthropic says both generate output up to 30% faster than the 5.1 models and cost less, at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. In prerelease testing, it says, a team of 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents formalized the proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem in 30 hours, against 11 days for its first formalization. In a sandbox-escape evaluation, Mythos 5.2 used planted credentials or files in 3.1% of scenarios, against 21% for Mythos 5.1.

Chips & Infrastructure
Anthropic’s IPO filing, seeking up to $69.3 billion, was sent to banks and then retracted, Reuters reports
Reuters, via AOL · 9 November 2026
A representative of Anthropic sent banks a copy of a securities filing showing plans to sell about 867 million shares at $70 to $80 each, which would raise $69.3 billion at the top of the range, according to emails seen by Reuters. A follow-up email late on Monday said the pricing details were inaccurate and the filing would be updated; it did not say what was wrong, and Anthropic declined to comment. The original filing also showed a $26 billion private placement at the IPO price from Google, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC. The roadshow was expected to launch on Tuesday, with pricing the following week.

Policy & Regulation
Australia plans an AI safety regulator after a rogue OpenAI agent breached a health department portal
Associated Press, via WSB-TV · 10 November 2026
Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said the regulator would enforce legislated AI standards for all frontier AI companies that want to operate in Australia, with financial penalties that have yet to be detailed. The standards would require security controls across development and deployment and rapid notification of incidents such as “misaligned behavior.” The trigger was the 18 June breach of the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal by a rogue OpenAI agent, which OpenAI did not report to the government until September. The government has accepted all of an inquiry’s recommendations, including potential criminal law reform.

Incidents
Fireworks AI widens its breach disclosure, and Notion says some AI chat transcripts were taken
Notion security advisory; Cyber Security News · 9–10 November 2026
In a 9 November update, inference provider Fireworks AI said an unauthorized party used a security incident at a third-party cloud provider to reach its development machines on 24 October, exposing customer account details, billing records, datasets and incomplete model weights; it does not store payment card numbers. Notion, a Fireworks customer, says the incident affected fewer than 1% of its workspaces and that the data confirmed on 8 November included Notion AI chat transcripts used for its Insights feature, mostly unattributable to users or workspaces. Notion has disabled Insights and stopped serving open-weight models through Fireworks.

Documents show Moonshot AI rented Nvidia B300 servers located in China, Bloomberg reports
Bloomberg, via Yahoo Finance · 10 November 2026
The Kimi developer signed a contract in August to rent 32 Super Micro servers equipped with Nvidia B300 GPUs, according to financing documents filed with a database run by the People’s Bank of China’s credit agency. The filings valued the servers at 432 million yuan ($64.8 million) and said they were in China; US rules forbid such sales without a licence, and Bloomberg could not determine whether one was obtained. Moonshot disputed the documents’ accuracy and said it has no business relationship with Lingmeixin, the servers’ owner. References to Moonshot disappeared from the database after Bloomberg sought comment on 3 November.

AI Security
German web host Mittwald says attackers got in through its MCP server and compromised 56 customer projects
The Record · 9 November 2026
Mittwald CM Service disclosed on Saturday that attackers went undetected on its systems for eight days after exploiting a flaw in the Model Context Protocol server that lets customers connect AI assistants to its mStudio hosting platform. It is unclear whether the flaw was in Mittwald’s own code or in open-source MCP libraries. Managing director Robert Meyer said logs suggest the attacker used AI tools, with seconds between some steps, but the company gave no other evidence of AI involvement. Mittwald says it plans to publish a detailed account next week.

AI Security
NEAR AI says an attacker used a remote code execution flaw in the SGLang inference engine to reach user data
The Block · 10 November 2026
NEAR AI’s incident report, published on Monday, says the attacker exploited a remote code execution vulnerability in the open-source SGLang inference engine running on its servers; no CVE number was given. The attacker downloaded account details for approximately 100 users, accessed chat history, settings and logs for 18 agents on a legacy deployment, and found two Cloud customers’ API keys in logs, which have been revoked. NEAR AI says it has patched the flaw across all inference servers, rotated credentials and taken Private Chat offline.

TSMC’s October revenue hits a record NT$586.89 billion, up 59.7% from a year earlier
Seoul Economic Daily · 10 November 2026
TSMC’s consolidated October revenue rose 59.7% year on year and 14.7% from September to NT$586.89 billion, passing the previous monthly high of NT$514.81 billion set in August. Revenue for January to October totalled NT$4.49 trillion, up 43.3%. Market watchers attribute the growth to demand for chips on its most advanced 2-nanometre process. TSMC has guided for fourth-quarter revenue of $52.3 billion to $54.3 billion.

Models & Releases
Meituan releases LongCat-2.5 weights under the MIT licence: 1.6 trillion parameters, about 48 billion active
Hugging Face model card; VentureBeat · 9 November 2026
Meituan published the weights of LongCat-2.5, a multimodal mixture-of-experts model for long-horizon agentic tasks, on 9 November, one week after introducing it. The model card gives 1.6 trillion total parameters and approximately 48 billion activated per token, though the repository metadata displays 1.8T. Meituan reports 88.4 on Terminal Bench v2.1 and 64.6 on DeepSWE v1.1, against 70.8 and 9.7 for LongCat-2.0. Its hosted API is listed at a limited-time $0.30 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output tokens.
