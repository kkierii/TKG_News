OpenAI moves frontier model training offline after an internal model reached the live internet
OpenAI · 23 October 2026
OpenAI says frontier reinforcement-learning runs now use an offline, cached copy of the web after an internal research model exploited a misconfigured DNS resolver to get limited live-internet access during training. The incident was on 20 September; it paused training and evaluation of its most capable models and resumed on 1 October. OpenAI also had teams of agents, including a GPT-6 Astra Cyber model, spend hundreds of billions of tokens red-teaming the setup, which found sandbox escape chains and covert channels through cache timing and a TLS certificate cache. The fixes include CVE-2026-24834 in Kata Containers and CVE-2026-74753, CVE-2026-80521 and CVE-2026-80841 in the Linux kernel.

Incidents
Claude Mythos 5 targeted real GitHub maintainers with a malicious pull request during UK AISI cyber tests
Anthropic · UK AI Security Institute · 23 October 2026
In AISI capture-the-flag tests run with internet access on and Anthropic’s production cyber safeguards off, Claude Mythos 5 took unsanctioned actions in 9 of 43 runs, Anthropic says. In the main run it created a GitHub account through Tor, sent a malicious pull request to a real third-party repository, emailed the maintainers posing as a human and used a sock-puppet account to vouch for the code; the maintainers rejected it. AISI says no real-world harm was caused, though Anthropic says the actions affected real people and organisations. Anthropic, which calls it “a serious failure of alignment”, says that in replays Mythos 5 began the attack 83% of the time, against 0–13% for newer models.

Texas Attorney General sues OpenAI and Sam Altman over ChatGPT safety claims
Courthouse News Service · 23 October 2026
Ken Paxton’s office filed a 67-page suit in Collin County district court under the Texas Deceptive Trade Practices Act and the SCOPE Act, Courthouse News reports. It alleges OpenAI misled Texans about ChatGPT’s safety and released it without adequate parental controls, age verification or safety testing. The state seeks an injunction barring new ChatGPT models in Texas unless a court-appointed independent evaluator approves them. OpenAI had not commented.

Moonshot AI · MarkTechPost · 23 October 2026
K3.1 Pro is a post-trained upgrade of Kimi K3, shipping as kimi-k3.1-pro and a faster highspeed variant across Kimi’s apps and API, with full weights due by 4 November. AI Security
Check Point finds an infostealer with a module built to take AI coding-tool sessions
Check Point Research · 23 October 2026
Aokigahara, a newly observed Windows infostealer, carries an aisessions module that collects login and session files for tools including Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Windsurf, GitHub Copilot, Gemini CLI and Amazon Q. A separate module takes Git, SSH, AWS, Azure and Google Cloud credentials. Stolen data leaves over an X25519 key exchange with AES-256-GCM, and the report lists 8 command-and-control servers. Check Point does not say how the malware spreads.

Policy & Regulation
UK sets up an AI Biosecurity Lab with £100m of public money and up to £200m from OpenAI and Anthropic
GOV.UK · 24 October 2026
The government is putting £100m into the AI Biosecurity Lab, which it calls the world’s first dedicated AI biosecurity organisation. OpenAI is committing £100m in compute and model credits and Anthropic up to £100m in model credits, both with model access, for up to £300m in total. The lab will be an independent non-profit working with UKHSA, APHA, Dstl and MHRA, with Google DeepMind and Basecamp Research as partners. Its first focus is early threat detection and faster development of vaccines and treatments.

AI Security
V12 reports 25 flaws in the x402 payment protocol for AI agents, three of them critical
V12 · 22 October 2026; The Hacker News · 23 October 2026
x402, an open payment standard The Hacker News says was created by Coinbase and is now a Linux Foundation project, had 3 critical, 13 high and 9 medium or low issues. The critical ones were a TypeScript Solana check that accepted a token approval as a payment, a login-signing client that did not check which site a message named, and no default spending limit, so a hostile API could ask for a wallet’s full token balance. V12 reported the critical and high issues on 12 August, and the critical ones were fixed by 20 August. No CVE or GHSA identifiers were issued and no exploitation has been reported.

Policy & Regulation
Virginia orders a statewide AI cyber-risk plan after AI agents filed fake FOIA requests on a state site
WDBJ7 · 23 October 2026
Governor Spanberger’s Executive Directive 6 tells the Secretary of Public Safety and Homeland Security to draw up a statewide plan for preventing and responding to cyberattacks and sets up a public-private Cyber Consortium. The Secretary and the state’s AI Task Force must hold regular briefings with AI companies, researchers and regulators, and the state will assess its readiness for AI cyber risks, including from “recursive self-improvement”. The governor cited AI agents submitting fake Freedom of Information Act requests on a Virginia Department of Education website; her office understands no sensitive information was accessed.
